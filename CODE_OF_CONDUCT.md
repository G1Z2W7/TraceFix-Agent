# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Contributors must:

- discuss code and decisions without attacking people;
- give specific, testable review feedback;
- respect privacy and remove sensitive data from examples;
- credit prior work and disclose relevant conflicts;
- accept a maintainer decision after the decision and rationale are recorded.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disclosure of private information, and repeated disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/G1Z2W7/TraceFix-Agent/security/advisories/new). Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban an account when needed to protect contributors and the project.
